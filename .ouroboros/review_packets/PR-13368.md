## Problem and intended behavior

Documents that MySQL's `DOUBLE(M,D)`, `REAL(M,D)`, and `FLOAT(M,D)` forms are deprecated, non-standard extensions and recommends omitting precision and scale. It also improves the error raised when only one of `precision` or `scale` is supplied, and adds regression coverage for all four invalid argument combinations.
